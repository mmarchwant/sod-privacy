Privacy Policy for Scroll of Dragons

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Scroll of Dragons ("the game") is an offline, single-player game for Android published by Marchevo ("we"). Contact: marchevo.it@gmail.com.

1. Summary

We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not need an internet connection to be played.

2. What the game stores on your device

Your game progress (hero, cards, town, settings, story and expedition state) is stored in a save file in the game's private storage on your phone. It contains game data only, not your name, email, location or device identifiers. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the game removes it from the phone.

3. Android backup

Android may back up the game's save file to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup) and restore it when you move to a new phone or reinstall the game. This is done by Android, under your Google account and your device's backup settings, not by us. We do not receive the backup and cannot read it. You can turn it off in your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Backup) or delete it there. Google's handling of backups is described in Google's own privacy policy.

4. Purchases

The game is free to start. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks the full version. The purchase is made through Google Play Billing: Google processes your payment, and your payment details (card, address) are handled by Google and never reach us. The game asks Google Play whether the full version is owned: when you buy, when you press "Restore purchase", and at start-up when you are online. The game stores the result on your device so that you can keep playing offline. We do not receive your purchase history beyond the sales and order reports Google Play provides to developers, which do not give us your identity beyond what Google Play shows in its console. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Network use

The game connects to the internet only to buy or restore the purchase through Google Play. It contacts no other service and loads nothing from the internet while you play.

6. Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no user-generated content and no ads.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google (your Google account, backups, payments) use Google's account tools.

8. Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and the date above before the update is released.
